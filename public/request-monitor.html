Coverage, privacy and retention

This backend instance only. Minute summaries cover up to seven days as collected and reset on restart. Boundary minute buckets can include up to 60 seconds outside a filter. Detailed rankings use the latest 3,000 events and may be truncated. No monitoring database queries, writes or provider probes.

SQL durations include driver/network time. No SQL values, bodies, headers, signed URLs, credentials or customer identifiers are retained. Tenant IDs come from authenticated context; unknown paths are masked. Direct browser-to-R2 transfers, Cloudflare edge traffic, other instances and provider accounts are outside coverage. Reconnects and physical print delivery are not reliably measured.

Root only. Viewer streams expire after 15 minutes and must reconnect. Monitoring does not change ordering, printing, or other business rules.
